中文图片简介

研究区位于甘肃省武威市中国农业大学石羊河实验站。左图标示实验站在中国及甘肃省的位置，右图根据研究小组提供的卫星影像展示实际试验田及周边环境，橙色轮廓标出膜下滴灌（DI）玉米试验田。根据原始试验资料，DI观测区规模为400 m × 200 m，覆膜率为75%，为2019—2022年玉米水分过程模拟与灌溉制度优化提供田间背景。

English caption

Figure X. Location of the study station and the drip-irrigated maize field. The Shiyanghe Experimental Station is located in Wuwei, Gansu Province, China. The left panel shows the reported station location, and the right panel illustrates the experimental field and its surroundings based on the satellite image supplied by the research team. The orange outline identifies the DI field. The original experimental description reports a field size of 400 m × 200 m and 75% plastic-film coverage.
